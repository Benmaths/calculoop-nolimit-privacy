CalcuLoop NoLimit — Privacy Policy

BenMaths · Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Who we are and how to contact us

CalcuLoop NoLimit is a mental math game developed by BenMaths. This policy explains how information is used in the Android application `com.benmaths.calculoop.nolimit`.

Data controller: Benaud Fabien, operating under the name BenMaths..

For privacy questions, or requests concerning your game data, contact fabben888@gmail.com. Do not send your password or payment card details.

2. Playing and signing in

You can play the solo game modes without signing in to Google Play Games. Online features, including rankings, duels, Weekly League, the online profile and the credits/shop features, require sign-in.

The app uses Google Play Games and Firebase Authentication to identify your account. You do not need to create a separate password for CalcuLoop. Authentication creates a Firebase account identifier associated with your sign-in.

3. Information processed

Depending on the features you use, the app processes:

  • Google Play Games and Firebase account identifiers and the display name provided by Play Games.
  • The nickname you choose in CalcuLoop, an optional country code/flag, and nickname moderation status. A selected flag is your choice, not a measurement of your location.
  • Game scores, records, weekly cumulative scores, Adventure progression and game-related timestamps.
  • Duel participant identifiers, names, results, status and deadlines.
  • Your credit balance, daily/weekly reward markers, owned cosmetic items and equipped skins, buttons and digit styles.
  • Nickname reports: the reporting account identifier, the reported player identifier and displayed name, report date, and notification-processing information.
  • Local settings and records on your device, including sound preferences, selected equipment, saved progress and pending score submissions.
  • Information you voluntarily include when contacting support, such as your email address and message.

Service providers may process technical information such as network addresses and service logs to deliver and protect their services. See the service-provider information below.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Account identifiers and game data are used to provide the online game features you request: saving progress, presenting rankings, running duels, managing credits and cosmetic ownership, and recovering records. The legal basis is performance of the service requested by the player.

Nickname reports, moderation records and relevant technical logs are used to keep public names appropriate, investigate problems and protect the service. The legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in maintaining a functioning and respectful game service.

Information supplied to support is used to answer your request. Any processing subject to a legal obligation is limited to the applicable obligation.

5. What other players can see

CalcuLoop's main Ranking and Weekly League use Firebase. They do not rely on your Google Play Games profile being public for a new score to appear in CalcuLoop's own rankings.

Other players can see the nickname/display name, optional flag, scores and rank displayed in those rankings. Duel information includes participant names and results. Choose a nickname that does not reveal information you want to keep private.

Existing records may include a previously used display name. Your private support messages and reporting account identity are not displayed in the ranking interface.

An older Play Games personal record may be retrieved to restore your record or link historical game data. Older app versions may have submitted scores to Google Play Games leaderboards, which are governed by Google's settings and policies.

6. Nickname reports and moderation

Players can report an inappropriate nickname from supported ranking screens. Reports are stored in Firebase and reviewed by the developer. A report notification containing player identifiers and the reported nickname may be sent to the developer's mailbox through Gmail.

Following review, the developer may replace an inappropriate nickname with a neutral name and restrict further nickname changes. The app then displays “Your nickname has been restricted.” Reporting does not itself deduct credits or automatically ban the reported player.

7. Storage and service providers

Local game settings and some progress are stored on your device. Online account-linked data is stored in Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Firebase Cloud Functions processes server-side tasks, including moderation notifications.

The services used include Google Play Games, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Google's supporting infrastructure, plus Gmail for moderation notifications and support correspondence. The public policy page is hosted through GitHub Pages; visiting it is also subject to GitHub's privacy practices.

Google and GitHub may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Their applicable privacy notices and contractual transfer safeguards describe how they handle international processing. Information about the safeguards applicable to Firebase services is available in its privacy information and data processing terms.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

8. Credits and purchases

The app has a cosmetic shop using virtual credits. Credit balances, owned items and equipped items are saved online. These virtual-credit transactions exist even while purchases with real money are not yet available.

When you purchase a credit pack offered in the app, payment is handled by Google Play. Purchase-related information, such as product identifiers, purchase tokens, verification status and credit allocation, is processed to validate the purchase, deliver credits and prevent duplicate allocation. The developer does not receive your full payment card details through purchase verification. Availability and prices are shown in Google Play's purchase interface. Refunds are subject to Google Play's applicable procedures and consumer rights.

9. Advertising and Analytics

CalcuLoop NoLimit does not currently display advertisements.

The current app does not use an advertising or audience analytics SDK. Google and Firebase may process operational service logs to run and protect their services.

10. Retention

Game profiles, balances, cosmetic ownership and progression are retained to maintain the account-linked game service and preserve the player's progress. Removing the app does not automatically remove online records. Local data is removed when the app's stored data is cleared, subject to the device's backup/restore settings.

Completed and expired duels are scheduled for deletion after 12 calendar months from the completion date recorded by the server. For older duels without a reliable completion date, this period begins when the retention system first identifies them as finished. Cleanup runs periodically, so deletion may take several additional days.

Nickname reports are scheduled for deletion 12 months after their creation. Related Gmail notifications are manually deleted after 12 months. Records needed for an ongoing dispute or legal obligation may be retained until that need ends. Deleting a report does not remove an independently recorded nickname restriction.

Account-linked profiles, rankings, progression, balances and cosmetic ownership remain stored to preserve progress until deletion is requested and processed or the service ends. Historical weekly standings are currently retained as game history and are included when an account-data deletion request is reviewed. Support correspondence is retained while the request and any necessary follow-up remain unresolved, and deleted when no longer needed. Provider operational logs and any provider backups follow their configured or contractual retention policies.

11. Your choices and rights

You can change your CalcuLoop nickname unless it has been restricted, and choose or remove your flag in Profile. You can change sound settings and play solo without signing in. Changing a visible nickname is not the same as deleting your account data.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent. Contact fabben888@gmail.com to exercise these rights; we may request reasonable information to identify your account and avoid deleting another player's data.

For users covered by the GDPR, you may complain to your local supervisory authority, including the French CNIL: https://www.cnil.fr/.

Deleting CalcuLoop data does not itself delete your Google account or data independently held by Google. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

12. Children's privacy

CalcuLoop is a mental math game. Google Play Games features are subject to Google's account eligibility, parental controls and service terms.

CalcuLoop NoLimit is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not intended for children under 13. Google account eligibility and any parental authorization requirements depend on the player’s country and applicable law.

If you believe a child has supplied information that should be removed, contact the developer.

13. Security

The app uses authentication and the access controls configured for its online services. No system can guarantee absolute security. Data access controls and purchase verification should be reviewed as the service changes.

Do not include confidential information in a public nickname or share passwords with the developer.

14. Changes

This policy may change when the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at https://benmaths.github.io/calculoop-nolimit-privacy/ and its update date will be revised. Material changes should be communicated as required by applicable law.
