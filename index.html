Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

CalcuLoop NoLimit is a free mental math game developed by BenMaths.

CalcuLoop NoLimit does not require you to create a separate CalcuLoop account.

To use the game, you sign in through Google Play Games Services. Google Play Games provides the identity used to associate your scores with your player profile.

Depending on your Google Play Games account and settings, the information made available to the game may include:

  • Your Google Play Games player identifier.
  • Your Play Games display name.
  • Your game scores and leaderboard information.
  • Other profile information required by Google Play Games Services.

CalcuLoop NoLimit does not ask you to provide your name, email address, telephone number, postal address, or other personal information directly.

2. Google Play Games Services

CalcuLoop NoLimit uses Google Play Games Services to authenticate players and provide leaderboard functionality.

Your Play Games identity is used to:

  • Sign you in to the game.
  • Associate your scores with your player profile.
  • Submit your scores to the CalcuLoop NoLimit leaderboard.
  • Display your position and score in the leaderboard.

Google processes information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy .

3. Leaderboards and Scores

When you complete a game, your score may be submitted to the CalcuLoop NoLimit Google Play Games leaderboard.

Leaderboard information may be visible to other players according to Google Play Games settings and the rules of the service. This may include your Play Games display name, score, rank, and related leaderboard information.

CalcuLoop NoLimit does not publish your email address or other private contact information on the leaderboard.

4. Data Storage

CalcuLoop NoLimit does not operate its own user account system or advertising database.

The application does not provide a separate cloud save system for game progress.

Leaderboard data and player identity information used by Google Play Games are handled through Google's services.

5. Advertising

CalcuLoop NoLimit does not contain advertisements.

The application does not use advertising SDKs and does not collect information for advertising or personalized advertising purposes.

6. Purchases

CalcuLoop NoLimit does not contain in-app purchases or paid digital items.

7. Analytics and Tracking

CalcuLoop NoLimit does not use third-party advertising trackers.

The application does not use tracking technologies to build advertising profiles about users.

8. Children's Privacy

CalcuLoop NoLimit is a general-purpose mental math game.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Because the game uses Google Play Games Services, the availability and processing of Play Games features may also be subject to Google's age requirements, parental controls, and privacy policies.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to limit the collection and processing of user information.

CalcuLoop NoLimit itself does not request sensitive personal information from users.

Information handled through Google Play Games Services is protected and processed by Google according to Google's security and privacy practices.

10. Third-Party Services

Google Play Games Services

Google Play Games Services is provided by Google LLC.
